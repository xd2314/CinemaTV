# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss behavior and evidence, not personal traits. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publishing private credentials or device data are unacceptable.

Keep technical criticism constructive and relevant. Respect maintainers' time and the owner's control of their devices. Never pressure someone to bypass safeguards or run a destructive experiment.

Maintainers may edit/remove unsafe or abusive content, close discussions and restrict participation. Report concerns through the repository's private reporting channel when available; do not repost private material publicly. The repository owner must configure a private contact before enabling public participation; see docs/PUBLISHING.md.
